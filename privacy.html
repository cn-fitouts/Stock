Privacy policy

CN Fitouts internal tools (Orders & Stock, Part Finder, Client Preview publishing). Last updated 26 October 2026.

Who we are

CN Fitouts is a joinery and fitout company in Melbourne, Australia. These tools are used only by CN Fitouts staff to manage our own orders, stock and job files.

What the Google connection is used for

Our publishing tool signs in to Google only to update and deploy our own Google Apps Script project (scopes: Apps Script projects and deployments). It does not read Gmail, Drive files, contacts or any other Google data, and it is not offered to anyone outside CN Fitouts.

What data we keep

  • Order, delivery and stock records for our business, stored in our own Google Sheet.
  • The name a staff member types on their device, recorded against stock movements.

We do not collect data from the public, we do not sell or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Keeping it safe and removing it

Access tokens are stored only on the CN Fitouts office computer. You can revoke the connection at any time from your Google Account under Security > Third-party connections.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the CN Fitouts office.
